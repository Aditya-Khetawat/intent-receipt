# Rules re-check

Checked 2026-11-07 against the live Devpost pages: `/rules`, `/details/faqs`, `/details/aggrid`, `/updates`.
The official rules prevail over `HACKATHON_BRIEF.md`.

## Matches the brief

- Submission deadline **Thu Nov 12, 2026, 12:00 pm Pacific**. Judging Dec 1 (2:00 pm PT) to Dec 15 (8:00 am PT). Winners around Dec 21.
- Must integrate the PayPal developer platform (free sandbox) **and** an AI tool/model; "PayPal integration is central".
- Working build required: hosted URL **or** complete run instructions in the repo. A mockup or non-functional demo does not qualify.
- Public GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket repo with an open-source license **visible in the About section**.
- Video under 3 minutes, public on YouTube, shows the project working, no third-party trademarks or copyrighted music.
- Project must stay free and available to judges until the Judging Period ends (Dec 15).
- Judging: Stage 1 pass/fail (theme + required APIs). Stage 2: Technological Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Innovation/Idea, Presentation, equally weighted; ties broken in that order. Judging may use automated AI analysis.
- Eligibility exclusions match (Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, other OFAC-sanctioned places; sponsor/administrator staff and families). India is not excluded.

## Differences from the brief

1. **Prize combinations.** The rules say a project can win up to *one Grand Prize and one Sponsor Prize* **or** *one Honorable Mention and one Sponsor Prize*. The brief only mentioned "one main prize plus one sponsor prize". No change to the plan.
2. **AG Grid sponsor prize guidance.** The `/details/aggrid` page emphasises **AG Studio** (embedded dashboards) and the **Studio Agent Framework**, asking for "polish: custom widgets, theming, and layout tools, not just AG Grid or AG Charts on their own". We use AG Grid Community (themed with our tokens, custom cell renderers, quick filter, saved sort, live row updates). Competing seriously for the AG Grid prize would need an AG Studio trial key and a Studio dashboard; that is a stretch item and needs the owner to start the 45-day trial (still valid on Dec 15 if started on or after Nov 7).
3. **Sponsor tools must be selected on the submission form** to be considered for a sponsor prize (from the Nov 6 update). Select AG Grid and Render on the Devpost form.
4. **Early eligibility check** was offered for submissions by Nov 5; that date has passed.
5. Updates recommend naming PayPal and AI explicitly in the description, "Built With" and video, and confirm an AI voiceover is fine.

Nothing found that conflicts with the project concept.
